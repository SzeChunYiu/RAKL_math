## What worked

The most effective step was binding **output and input topologies** theorem-by-theorem. Albritton–Barker local compactness outputs strong `L^3_loc` velocity convergence and weak `L^{3/2}_loc` pressure convergence; their global ancient Liouville theorem consumes a backward sequence with uniformly bounded global `L^3`. Critical-element/profile machinery consumes a bounded global critical-space sequence. Putting those signatures side by side exposed the missing map without inventing a PDE inequality.

Counterexample-first topology checks then falsified a common implicit shortcut. Translation escape preserves global critical norm while disappearing on every fixed compact subset; orthogonal translation/dilation profiles can leak critical mass from the selected core. The pressure audit separately showed that local Calderón–Zygmund plus harmonic-remainder control is adequate for local persistence/epsilon regularity but is not a global pressure/tail identity. The self-similar audit prevented equation-class leakage: stationary Leray-profile theorems apply only after an exact similarity reduction.

## What failed

No mathematical contradiction inside the local compactness theory was found. The failure is **gluing/interface**, not local mathematics. The route failed when it attempted to feed local compactness directly into a global critical-element or global-L3 rigidity theorem.

The unique-continuation route was also blocked at verification: “backward uniqueness” cannot be treated as a free operator. Exact terminal-time, exterior/global, coefficient/regularity and domain hypotheses must be verified for the vorticity equation of the actual ancient limit.

No numerical experiment was used; the adversarial constructions are analytic/topological calibrations only.
