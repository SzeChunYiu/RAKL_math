## Expert cell and delegation

The same-context cell contained six roles: PDE scaling/endpoints, vorticity geometry, blow-up compactness, harmonic-analysis/nonlocality, conditional-regularity transfer, and adversarial verification/metrology.

The scaling pass tracked `omega_lambda=lambda^2 omega(lambda x,lambda^2t)`, dimensionless vorticity direction, Hölder-seminorm scaling, derivative loss, and the normalization endpoint at `omega=0`.

The geometry pass identified the exact consumer objects in Constantin–Fefferman, Miller, and Barker–Prange and refused to treat a conditional regularity input as an inherited a priori bound.

The compactness pass held the producer stage to its registered strength rather than upgrading strong local velocity convergence to strong vorticity convergence.

The harmonic-analysis pass separated pressure from nonlocality: pressure cancels under curl, while the strain/Biot–Savart singular integral remains.

The transfer pass recorded the whole-space/half-space and finite-`I`/ODE-Type-I disanalogies.

The adversarial pass supplied the oscillatory divergence-free calibration and verified that it targets only the topology implication, not Navier–Stokes dynamics.

## What worked

The counterexample-first test worked exactly at the intended scope. On a periodic box,

`w_n=(0,n^{-1}sin(nx_1),0)`

is divergence free, tends strongly to zero in local `L^3`, and has uniformly bounded gradient `L^2`, while its curl direction flips between `+e_3` and `-e_3` on `O(n^{-1})` scales. This verifies a local representation fact: the compactness topology currently registered does not continuously transport a vorticity-direction modulus.

The source-signature audit also worked. Barker–Prange explicitly assumes vorticity-direction continuity in its half-space ODE-Type-I theorem; Miller explicitly assumes a scale-critical projected-vorticity bound and controlled plane field; the recent logarithmic-depletion preprint adds its own concentration and direction-space hypotheses. These are consumer inputs, not finite-`I` outputs.

The pressure audit prevented a false transfer of an old obstruction: pressure is absent after curl, but nonlocal strain remains.

## What failed

The attempted **direct inheritance route** failed. No registered estimate in the frozen finite-`I` fibre bridges strong local velocity compactness/natural weak derivative control to a source-ready vorticity-direction or projected-vorticity geometric quantity.

The failure category is primarily `LOCAL_MATHEMATICAL_REPRESENTATION_INTERFACE`: one derivative is lost and normalization at vorticity zeros is unstable. A separate `CONTEXT_DIFFERENCE` blocks direct use of the half-space ODE-Type-I analogue.

This is not a new local-to-global gluing failure. `NS-B1a3` already carries that separate global-critical-tightness obstruction. In `NS-B1a3b` the route stops earlier, at local representation/inheritance.

The cycle did **not** show that additional Navier–Stokes dynamics cannot imply geometry. It only falsified topology-only inheritance and localized the new estimate that would be required.
