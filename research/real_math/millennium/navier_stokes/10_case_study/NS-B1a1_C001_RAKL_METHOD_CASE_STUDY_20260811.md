## What worked

The exact scaling audit localized a clean boundary. Linear cutoff, cubic convection and pressure-work terms all have physical scale `O(R)` when `A,C,D,E` are bounded; after normalization by the natural energy scale they are `O(1)`. The nested-dyadic stress test also made explicit that physical expenditures proportional to `R_j` or `R_j^2` can sum finitely over infinitely many descendants.

The v3 success/failure memory changed routing materially. `F-NS-B1a-C001-PRESSURE-SUMMABILITY` prevented repeating the instantaneous pressure-divergence idea. The cross-problem local-to-global warning `F-C024-FRACTIONAL-INTEGRALITY-GAP` motivated attacking the assembly map rather than strengthening another local scalar. The audited terminal-scale defect in arXiv:2606.07875v1 prevented importing a smallest-scale/well-foundedness shortcut. The reusable cross-problem tool `T-XM-ROOT-BRIDGE-STABILITY-AUDIT` supplied the exact surrogate/root/bridge/falsifier pattern under a target-specific DifferenceWitness.

## What failed

The naive route `bounded standard local-energy ledger at each scale -> summable or terminal descendant charge` failed. This is not a local Navier–Stokes mathematical failure: the source-valid local-energy inequality and finite Type-I bounds remain intact. The failure is **local-to-global gluing/assembly**. Scale invariance removes the positive scale power needed by a naive normalized summation, and nested scales need not be disjoint.
