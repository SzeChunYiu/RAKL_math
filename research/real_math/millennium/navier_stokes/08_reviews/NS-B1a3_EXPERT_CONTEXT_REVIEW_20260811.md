## Deliberation and delegation

The local-energy specialist accepts the Albritton–Barker compactness interface only at its stated strength: after uniform local `L^3` velocity and `L^{3/2}` pressure control, a subsequence converges strongly in `L^3_loc` for velocity and weakly in `L^{3/2}_loc` for pressure. Persistence of singularities preserves a nontrivial singular core. This does **not** state global `L^3` convergence or tail tightness.

The critical-element specialist notes that the Gallagher–Koch–Planchon program begins with boundedness in an explicit global critical Lebesgue/Besov topology and then resolves translation/dilation loss of compactness by profile decomposition. A finite Albritton–Barker `I` bound is a local scale-invariant cylinder supremum. Importing critical-element minimality before constructing a global critical state space would therefore reverse the logical direction of the source method.

The pressure specialist checks the exact persistence proof interface: pressure is split into a localized singular-integral piece and a harmonic remainder. The local argument controls the harmonic remainder on smaller cylinders, but that is not a global far-field pressure identification. Consequently local pressure compactness cannot be silently upgraded to a global pressure/tail certificate.

The vorticity specialist blocks a generic phrase such as “apply backward uniqueness.” The source rigidity route is conditional on an exact critical/terminal setting. The vorticity equation has drift/stretching coefficients generated by the velocity gradient, and any unique-continuation theorem must be matched to the actual regularity, exterior control, time slice and domain of the limiting solution. Finite `I` plus local convergence alone does not discharge those obligations.

The geometry specialist argues against treating the compactness-route failure as evidence against geometric depletion. Vorticity-direction coherence is an equation-specific structural coordinate, not another global scalar budget. It remains a live sibling route, but must itself be frozen in a separate child context with its exact source hypotheses before a candidate is formed.

The adversarial reviewer supplies four falsifiers: (i) translation escape preserves global norm while producing zero on each fixed compact set; (ii) orthogonal translation/dilation profiles can carry critical mass outside the selected local core; (iii) harmonic pressure remainders retain far-field information not fixed by local Calderón–Zygmund convergence; and (iv) an exact self-similar substitution changes the equation to the stationary Leray system, so stationary-profile Liouville theorems do not classify arbitrary ancient trajectories.
