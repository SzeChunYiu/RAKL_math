## Executive finding

The existing Type-I compactness output cannot be used **by topology alone** to transport a vorticity-direction/coherence hypothesis into the ancient limit. The map

`u -> omega=curl u -> xi=omega/|omega|`

sits one derivative beyond local strong `L^3` velocity convergence and then applies a normalization that is unstable without nonvanishing/strong convergence. An explicit smooth compactly supported divergence-free sequence has `w_n -> 0` strongly in `L^3`, uniformly bounded first-derivative energy, and order-one vorticity whose direction rotates on scale `1/n`. A second exact periodic Navier–Stokes family shows that exact PDE evolution plus finite kinetic energy also does not by itself produce a scale-uniform direction-coherence modulus.

This prunes the naive bridge

`finite-I blow-up compactness -> inherited vorticity-direction coherence -> geometric depletion theorem`

unless a **new source-valid derivative/orientation stability certificate** is inserted. It does not refute Constantin–Fefferman, does not refute a dynamics-specific coherence estimate, and does not exclude Type-I or Type-II blow-up.

## Falsifier A: local R3 compactness topology

Let `chi in C_c^infty(R^3)` equal one on a ball and define

`A_n = n^{-2} chi(x) (0, sin(n x_1), cos(n x_1))`,  
`w_n = curl A_n`.

Then `div w_n=0` identically and `w_n` is smooth and compactly supported. Every first derivative of `A_n` is `O(n^{-1})+O(n^{-2})`, hence

`||w_n||_L3 <= C_chi n^{-1}`.

Every first derivative of `w_n` contains at most two derivatives of `A_n`, with the leading phase factor `n^2` canceled by `n^{-2}`, hence

`||grad w_n||_L2 <= C_chi`

uniformly in `n`.

On a smaller core where `chi=1`,

`w_n=(0,n^{-1} sin(n x_1),n^{-1} cos(n x_1))`,

and therefore

`omega_n=curl w_n=(0,sin(n x_1),cos(n x_1))`.

Thus `|omega_n|=1` there, so no zero-vorticity singularity is being used to create the defect. The normalized direction is exactly `xi_n=omega_n`. At two core points with `x_1=0` and `y_1=pi/(2n)`, the directions are orthogonal while the spatial separation is `pi/(2n)`. Consequently, for every `alpha>0`,

`[xi_n]_{C^alpha(core)} >= c_alpha n^alpha`.

In particular, the directions are not equicontinuous and have no strongly compact orientation field in any positive Hölder topology, even though velocity converges strongly in `L^3` and first-derivative energy stays bounded.

### Scope of falsifier A

`w_n` is not claimed to solve Navier–Stokes. It is a precise falsifier of the **functional-topology implication** that the stored velocity compactness plus derivative-energy bound alone transports `xi`. Exact PDE structure could still supply an additional compactness mechanism; proving such a mechanism is now an explicit residual.

## Falsifier B: exact periodic Navier–Stokes calibration

On `T^3=(R/2pi Z)^3`, fix `a>0` and define for `t>=0`

`u_n(x,t)=(a/n)e^{-n^2 t}(0,sin(n x_1),cos(n x_1))`,  
`p_n=constant`.

The field is divergence-free. Because its first component is zero and all components depend only on `x_1`,

`(u_n·grad)u_n=0`.

Also `partial_t u_n=-n^2 u_n` and `Delta u_n=-n^2 u_n`, so

`partial_t u_n - Delta u_n + (u_n·grad)u_n + grad p_n = 0`.

This is therefore an exact smooth Navier–Stokes solution. Its vorticity is

`omega_n=a e^{-n^2t}(0,sin(n x_1),cos(n x_1))`.

At `t=0`,

`||u_n||_L2(T3)^2=(2pi)^3 a^2/n^2`,  
`||omega_n||_L2(T3)^2=(2pi)^3 a^2`,

while the normalized direction again rotates at frequency `n` and has positive-Hölder seminorm at least `c_alpha n^alpha`.

This exact-solution calibration demonstrates that finite kinetic energy and exact Navier–Stokes evolution, without a dangerous-scale/high-vorticity coherence mechanism, do not furnish a uniform direction-modulus estimate. The periodic domain, forward-decaying regime and absence of a singular sequence are explicit disanalogies; no theorem about the R3 Type-I ancient class is transferred from it.

## Scaling and units audit

For the R3 blow-up scaling

`u_r(x,t)=r u(x0+r x,t0+r^2t)`,  
`omega_r=r^2 omega(x0+r x,t0+r^2t)`.

Where vorticity is nonzero,

`xi_r(x,t)=xi(x0+r x,t0+r^2t)`.

Thus direction is dimensionless, but its spatial Hölder seminorm transforms as

`[xi_r]_{C^alpha(B_R)} = r^alpha [xi]_{C^alpha(B_{rR})}`.

Scale covariance of `xi` does **not** imply that a coherence modulus is generated by finite `I`, nor that it is stable under the stored compactness topology. The new obstruction is therefore not a units error; it is a regularity/topology mismatch.

## Constants and endpoint audit

- All constants in falsifier A depend only on the fixed cutoff `chi` and its first two derivatives, never on `n`.
- The vorticity magnitude on the core is exactly one, so normalization has no small-denominator defect there; the failure is genuinely derivative/topology driven.
- No positive Hölder endpoint survives: the lower bound grows as `n^alpha` for every `alpha>0`.
- This cycle makes no claim at the `alpha=0` endpoint beyond boundedness of the direction itself.
- The exact periodic calibration allows any fixed amplitude `a>0`; increasing `a` can place the family above any fixed nonzero vorticity threshold at `t=0` while kinetic energy still decays like `n^{-2}`.

## Pressure / nonlocality audit

Pressure is not used to manufacture the counterexample. In the periodic exact family the nonlinear term vanishes and pressure may be constant. Therefore a pressure estimate cannot repair the specific logical gap `strong velocity topology -> stable normalized curl` without introducing additional derivative information. Pressure/far-field control remains a separate later gluing obligation.

## Derivative-loss and circular-bootstrap audit

A circular route would say: finite `I` gives local strong `L^3` velocity convergence; the limit therefore inherits vorticity-direction coherence; the inherited coherence then supplies regularity/rigidity. The first implication is exactly what the falsifiers show is not available from the registered convergence modes. Any repair must be proved independently, for example by:

1. strong local compactness of vorticity in a topology sufficient for the geometric observable;
2. a quantitative nondegeneracy/high-vorticity localization plus orientation compactness theorem;
3. a direct coherence estimate derived from exact Type-I dynamics that bypasses passage through `xi_n`;
4. a different geometric observable continuous under the actual compactness topology.

Using the desired geometric regularity conclusion to justify the strong derivative convergence would be circular and is rejected.
