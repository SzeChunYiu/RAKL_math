## Primary-source anchors

1. Pierre Deligne, *The Hodge conjecture*, Clay Mathematics Institute (2006): exact rational root scope.
2. Nikolay Buskin, *Every rational Hodge isometry between two K3 surfaces is algebraic*, arXiv:1510.02852; J. Reine Angew. Math. 755 (2019). For projective K3 surfaces, rational Hodge isometries of `H^2` have algebraic correspondence classes.
3. Eyal Markman, *Rational Hodge isometries of hyper-Kähler varieties of K3[n]-type are algebraic*, arXiv:2204.00516; Compos. Math. 160 (2024). In the projective case the Hodge isometries considered are induced by algebraic correspondences.
4. Yves André, *Pour une théorie inconditionnelle des motifs*, Publ. Math. IHÉS 83 (1996). Motivated cycles/motives give an unconditional enlarged correspondence category; this is not a general Chow-algebraization theorem.
5. Pierre Deligne, *Hodge cycles on abelian varieties*, LNM 900 (1982): absolute-Hodge rigidity on abelian varieties, not general Chow source existence.
6. Deepam Patel and Yilong Zhang, *Algebraicity of Hodge classes on some Generalized Prym Varieties*, arXiv:2506.13729, manuscript dated 2026-05-22. Geometric class-field-theory/Abel–Jacobi constructions produce explicit algebraic cycles spanning specified Weil-Hodge subspaces.
7. Ljudmila Kamenova and Abhinav Kumar, *Correspondences for hyperkähler varieties with large Picard numbers*, arXiv:2607.09622, submitted 2026-07-10. Certain known hyperkähler varieties with large Picard number are related by algebraic correspondences to abelian varieties.

## New discriminator

A proposed source-span route should be materialized as a `CorrespondenceSourceSpanCertificate` with:

`(target_class, generator_family, generator_algebraicity, source_class_algebraicity, closure_law, target_coverage_theorem, complement_hodge_zero_proof, alpha_reconstruction)`.

Classify each route:

- `CERTIFIED_GENERATOR_COVERAGE` — every generator/source has Chow authority and coverage is independently proved;
- `CERTIFIED_SUBSPACE_ONLY` — a proper Hodge subspace is covered, with exact scope;
- `SOURCE_PROBLEM_RELOCATED` — correspondence exists but the needed source Hodge class is not independently algebraic;
- `SOURCE_CATEGORY_RELAXED` — motivated/absolute/categorical source substitutes for Chow;
- `COVERAGE_OPEN_ROOT_LIKE` — generator algebraicity is known but target inclusion/span is equivalent in role to the unresolved Hodge image problem;
- `COMPLEMENT_NOT_PROVED_EMPTY` — residual Hodge component is discarded without an exact zero-Hodge theorem.

## Structural information gain

Buskin and Markman expose a stronger pattern than “find an algebraic correspondence”: a **restricted target morphism family is proved to lie in a geometrically algebraizable closure**. Patel–Zhang exposes a parallel span pattern for a specified Hodge subspace. The common research coordinate is therefore:

`certified algebraic generators -> closure theorem -> target coverage -> residual/complement audit`.

This does not solve the Hodge conjecture. It identifies two independent missing bridges for a generalization:

1. **generator existence/algebraicity** for a sufficiently rich family;
2. **coverage** of the arbitrary target Hodge class without assuming the root.

A third bridge appears when using a decomposition: **prove the complement has no rational Hodge classes**.

The calibration is a route discriminator, not a Hodge theorem.
