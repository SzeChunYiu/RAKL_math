The mathematical GLUE is exact in its stated parity-separated scope. For an even-even child graph edge, the mandatory row and column complement traces are disjoint from one another and from the old odd-odd slice. Decorating a parent pair `(A,B)` by those two traces leaves the decorated intersection exactly `i(A intersect B)`. Cylinder membership depends only on old-slice projection, so the decorated child pair has exactly the same lifted old load as `(A,B)`. Consequently any fresh relevant child semi-filter that can receive positive one-coordinate mass must contain the embedded intersection of every tight parent pair. A tight parent pair with empty intersection is an immediate blocker because a semi-filter cannot contain the empty set.

This contracts the residual. C041A asked for a fresh graph-realizable filter outside all saturated lifted-pair neighbourhoods. C041B shows that, for even-even witnesses, a necessary part of that search is entirely parent-side: compile the intersection family of tight parent constraints and demand that the new filter absorb it. The result is local and conditional but turns an unstructured child-neighbourhood search into a concrete obstruction object.
