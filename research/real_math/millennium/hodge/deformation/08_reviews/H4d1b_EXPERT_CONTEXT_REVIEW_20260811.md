# H4d1b same-context expert review

**Atom:** `H4d1b`  
**Scope:** source-bound first-order direct lifting of an already algebraic complete-intersection witness along Hodge-locus tangent directions.  
**Review authority:** `SAME_CONTEXT_ROLE_SEPARATED_ONLY / NOT_INDEPENDENT_PEER_REVIEW`.

The review cell is deliberately role separated. Each pass receives the same frozen source/context packet and does not create theorem authority.

## 1. VHS / Hodge-locus lead
**Background and role.** Hodge-theoretic deformation specialist. Checks that the base tangent object is genuinely the Hodge-locus tangent and not a geometric witness locus renamed after the fact.

**Finding.** In Kloosterman's complete-intersection source, the tangent space to the Hodge locus at the hypersurface is identified with the degree-`e` piece `I_e` of the Artinian-Gorenstein ideal generated by the complete-intersection equations `P_i` and complementary forms `Q_i`. This supplies an independently computed base-side target for the incidence lift.

**Strongest objection.** The identity is source-specific. It cannot be assumed for an arbitrary Hodge class or arbitrary family.

**Vote:** `ACCEPT_SOURCE_BOUND_CALIBRATION`.

## 2. Algebraic-cycle / incidence lead
**Background and role.** Hilbert/flag-Hilbert deformation specialist. Checks whether every allowed base tangent has an actual first-order pair deformation.

**Finding.** If `deltaF in I_e=(P_i,Q_i)_e`, degree compatibility gives
`deltaF=sum_i(P_i deltaQ_i + deltaP_i Q_i)`.
With `P_i(epsilon)=P_i+epsilon deltaP_i` and `Q_i(epsilon)=Q_i+epsilon deltaQ_i`,
`sum_i P_i(epsilon)Q_i(epsilon)=F+epsilon deltaF mod epsilon^2`.
Thus the dual-number complete intersection `Z_epsilon=(P_i(epsilon)=0)` is contained in `Y_epsilon=(F+epsilon deltaF=0)`. The first-order obstruction of the chosen pair therefore vanishes for every source Hodge tangent.

**Strongest objection.** This is a first-order incidence statement. Syzygies, flatness beyond dual numbers, and higher obstruction maps must be handled separately.

**Vote:** `ACCEPT_FIRST_ORDER_ONLY`.

## 3. Semiregularity / derived-obstruction lead
**Background and role.** Obstruction-theory specialist. Checks whether the route silently reintroduces the detector whose kernel caused H4d1/H4d1a to fail.

**Finding.** The positive calibration bypasses same-detector injectivity. It constructs a geometric preimage before invoking a detector, so the H4d1a no-go does not apply to this source calibration.

**Strongest objection.** Kloosterman explicitly notes the complete-intersection cycles in the source class are semiregular. Therefore this does not demonstrate that direct branch annihilation is strictly weaker than full semiregularity.

**Vote:** `ACCEPT_BUT_STRICT_WEAKER_CLAIM_BLOCKED`.

## 4. Special-family calibration lead
**Background and role.** Algebraic-geometry calibration specialist. Compares nearby solved settings and asks what source assumptions carry the result.

**Finding.** Bruzzo-Montoya supplies an independent toric pair/Noether-Lefschetz positive control under its own assumptions. The repeated pattern is a source theorem identifying the Hodge-persistence locus with a geometric pair-deformation locus, not an unrestricted principle for all cycles.

**Strongest objection.** Two special complete-intersection families do not establish transfer to arbitrary cycles.

**Vote:** `ACCEPT_AS_REPEATED_SPECIAL_FAMILY_PATTERN`.

## 5. Adversarial falsification lead
**Background and role.** Searches for the cheapest failure of the proposed bridge.

**Finding.** The cheapest source-level falsifier is a `deltaF` classified as Hodge-preserving but outside the incidence tangent image. Kloosterman's `T_Hodge=I_e=(P_i,Q_i)_e` identity eliminates that falsifier in the exact complete-intersection source. Movasati-style cases where a selected witness is rigid while a Hodge locus is positive-dimensional remain hostile controls against generalization.

**Strongest objection.** First-order image equality can coexist with higher-order obstruction. It also need not survive a change of witness category.

**Vote:** `PASS_SOURCE_FALSIFIER / HIGHER_ORDER_OPEN`.

## 6. Formal, novelty and RAKL-learning control
**Background and role.** Formal-methods and research-governance specialist. Checks statement binding, scope, novelty and whether accumulated negative experience changed the research move.

**Finding.** The cycle is a source-bound method calibration, not new mathematics. The prior verified first-order same-detector impossibility changes the active representation from detector faithfulness to incidence preimage. This is a qualitative memory-conditioned routing effect, but causal attribution to RAKL cannot be measured without a matched model-only/reset control.

**Strongest objection.** Calling the explicit factorization itself novel would be misleading because it is an extraction from the published source proof structure.

**Vote:** `ACCEPT_TRANSFER_NOVEL_METHOD_CASE / NO_THEOREM_NOVELTY`.

## Cell synthesis

The six passes agree on a narrow positive result:

`KLOOSTERMAN_SOURCE_FIRST_ORDER_DIRECT_LIFT_CALIBRATION = PASS`.

They also agree on two blockers before this can become a stronger H4d1b mechanism:

1. **Strict-weaker blocker.** Find a source-bound case with a nonzero Hodge-branch tangent and direct first-order witness lift where full semiregularity injectivity is independently absent or fails; otherwise the claimed weakening is not demonstrated.
2. **Higher-order blocker.** Freeze a separate Artin-order child and test whether the incidence lift can be extended compatibly beyond dual numbers.

The rational Hodge root, initial algebraicity, arbitrary-cycle generality, formal algebraization, monodromy/global continuation, novelty and independent review remain open.
