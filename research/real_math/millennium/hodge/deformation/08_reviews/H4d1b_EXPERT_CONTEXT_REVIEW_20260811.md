# H4d1b same-context expert review

**Atom:** `H4d1b`  
**Authority:** `SAME_CONTEXT_ROLE_SEPARATED_REVIEW / NOT_INDEPENDENT / PRE_CANDIDATE`

## Frozen question

For a fixed rational Hodge-locus branch `T` and chosen algebraic central witness `z0`, determine what first-order branch obstruction annihilation `delta_1|T=0` actually certifies, and whether it can be used as an all-order witness-propagation certificate.

## Role-separated passes

### 1. Hodge/VHS lead

**Evidence inspected.** H4d1/H4d1a packets; Kloosterman complete-intersection and intersecting-linear-space Hodge-locus papers; Dan's complete-intersection variational-Hodge result.

**Strongest objection.** Hodge persistence is a base-side statement. Even when branch tangent directions satisfy the first-order geometric compatibility required by a witness map, the Hodge locus has scheme structure and can be singular or nonreduced.

**Falsifier.** Compare any proposed first-order certificate against a source where reduced Hodge support and geometric support agree but the Hodge locus is nonreduced.

**Residual uncertainty.** The exact scheme structure of a general rational Hodge-locus branch is source- and family-dependent.

**Vote:** `REVISE`. Treat first order as a checkpoint, not propagation authority.

### 2. Algebraic-cycle / witness-moduli lead

**Evidence inspected.** H4d1 witness-projection calibration and special complete-intersection controls.

**Strongest objection.** A tangent vector in `T_{s0}T` lying in the image of `dq` says that the chosen witness has a lift over dual numbers. It does not produce compatible lifts over `C[epsilon]/(epsilon^{m+1})` or arbitrary Artin thickenings.

**Falsifier.** Demand an explicit small-extension lifting contract for the witness incidence map over `T`; if the argument only computes `dq`, it fails the all-order claim.

**Residual uncertainty.** In arbitrary higher codimension the correct witness object may be a Hilbert/Chow/derived moduli problem rather than one universal `W`.

**Vote:** `REVISE`.

### 3. Formal/scheme-theoretic deformation lead

**Evidence inspected.** Stacks Project formal-smoothness and small-extension lifting criteria.

**Strongest objection.** Formal smoothness is an infinitesimal lifting property over all relevant thickenings, not one tangent-surjectivity condition. Upgrading a dual-number calculation to formal dominance without obstruction control changes the statement.

**Falsifier.** Plant a higher-order obstruction after a first-order lift or use a nonreduced target calibration; a tangent-only certificate must return insufficient evidence.

**Residual uncertainty.** The appropriate representability/finiteness hypotheses for the chosen cycle functor must be supplied before importing scheme/algebraic-space smoothness criteria literally.

**Vote:** `BLOCK` any all-order claim from first-order evidence alone.

### 4. Adversarial Hodge-locus lead

**Evidence inspected.** Kloosterman's 2026 revision of arXiv:2401.10775, especially the `k=1` summary of Maclean/Dan and the special singular examples.

**Strongest objection.** Classical Noether-Lefschetz loci can be nonreduced even when their reductions equal a natural geometric locus. Therefore replacing the Hodge scheme by its reduced support can erase exactly the higher-order coordinate H4d1b needs to control.

**Falsifier.** A proposed formal certificate that is invariant under taking reduction is too weak unless an independent theorem proves the target Hodge branch reduced/smooth at the point.

**Residual uncertainty.** The cited `k=1` examples already show excess tangent dimension, so they do not establish the stronger same-point pattern `tangent equality + formal failure`. They only show that higher-order scheme structure is a genuine coordinate and reduced equality is insufficient.

**Vote:** `REVISE`, with this limitation explicit.

### 5. Method-transfer / cross-problem lead

**Evidence inspected.** H4d1a failure memory and the reachable-subspace analogy.

**Strongest objection.** The previous control-theory analogy was useful at first order but can itself cause overreach if linearized reachability is treated as nonlinear/all-order reachability.

**Falsifier.** Require the transferred principle to state a second-stage all-order validation obligation rather than reusing the first-order analogy as proof.

**Residual uncertainty.** There is no promoted cross-domain RAKL tool with validated Hodge applicability.

**Vote:** `ACCEPT` only as a proposal-compressing analogy.

### 6. Novelty/research-value and assurance lead

**Evidence inspected.** Existing Hodge route history and primary source controls.

**Strongest objection.** The mathematical fact that first-order deformation data do not equal formal smoothness is established background, not new mathematics. The research contribution here is route-pruning and a sharper decomposition of obligations.

**Falsifier.** If the cycle claims a new Hodge theorem, new formal-smoothness theorem, or root progress beyond scoped method calibration, reject it.

**Residual uncertainty.** Whether a source-specific H4d1b first-order vanishing theorem can be proved for a useful non-semiregular family remains open.

**Vote:** `ACCEPT` a source-bound calibration and child residual; `BLOCK` novelty/root authority.

## Synthesis

Consensus is that `delta_1|T=0` is worth retaining as a first-order checkpoint but must not be promoted to an all-order propagation condition. The cheapest next action is adversarial rather than inventive: freeze the distinction between dual-number liftability and formal/scheme-theoretic witness domination, calibrate it against a special positive variational-Hodge family and a nonreduced Hodge-locus hostile family, and open a separate higher-Artin child if the distinction survives.

The roles above are same-context analytical passes. They are not independent mathematical review.
