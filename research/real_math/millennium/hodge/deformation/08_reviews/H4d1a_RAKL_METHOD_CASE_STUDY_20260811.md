## Research method used

The cycle used a **contrastive source-bound interface audit** rather than theorem invention.

The working hypothesis was that full detector faithfulness might be unnecessarily strong if the Hodge branch reaches only a smaller obstruction envelope. The key methodological requirement was anti-tautological: define reachability from source-side witness geometry before looking at the desired vanishing.

The cycle therefore compared:

1. positive controls where the Hodge condition and witness deformation are genuinely coupled by source hypotheses;
2. a hostile control where the Hodge class moves but the originally chosen witness family is rigid;
3. a fresh base-side Hodge-locus description to test whether improved knowledge of `T` alone closes the witness interface.

## Expert-role findings

### VHS / Hodge-locus analyst

The base Hodge branch is a valid local object: it constrains when the flat class remains of type `(p,p)`. It does not identify which algebraic representative moves, or whether the selected representative moves at all.

### Algebraic-cycle / witness-moduli analyst

The missing object is not another Hodge equation. It is the moduli/deformation object of the selected witness category and its projection to the ambient deformation space. Replacement of one algebraic representative by another must be distinguished from persistence of the same witness.

### Semiregularity / obstruction analyst

Semiregular divisor settings remain useful positive controls: under strong source hypotheses, Hodge persistence can govern relative deformation. They do not establish the strict-weaker general mechanism sought by H4d1a.

### Special-family calibration analyst

Complete-intersection geometry supplies another positive restricted control. The rigid-selected-witness cubic family supplies the decisive hostile control and prevents a base-only definition of reachability.

### Adversarial falsification analyst

The falsified inference is:

`Hodge branch direction -> chosen witness moves in that direction`.

The falsifier does not attack the Hodge conjecture; it attacks a research representation that silently identifies a cohomology-class trajectory with a fixed witness trajectory.

### Formal assurance / method-learning analyst

The result occurs after the frozen strict packet and successful machine gate, so it may be recorded as an outcome-linked v3 episode. The new failure is still only an observation; it is not automatically a reusable obstruction or promoted lesson.

This is primarily a **representation + relation/gluing failure**, not a failure of local Hodge-locus mathematics.

## Local-to-global / gluing diagnosis

Two local sections can both be correct:

1. the flat class remains Hodge along `T`;
2. a chosen algebraic witness has its own deformation/obstruction theory.

They do not form a global witness-lifting conclusion until the interface is explicit. The next child must bind a witness object `W`, projection `pi:W->S`, representative identity/replacement semantics, and the relative geometry over `T`.

This suggests that some research failures currently recorded as “missing theorem” are more precisely **missing interface contracts**.

## Concrete framework-improvement hypothesis

**Hypothesis:** v3 fibres/gluing should carry explicit `LOCAL_SECTION_IDENTITY` and `INTERFACE_COVERAGE` semantics for conclusions that mention related-but-not-identical objects.

**Target surfaces:** `problem_fibre.py` and `gluing_learning.py`.

**Expected benefit:** prevent local claims from gluing when the bridge silently changes representative/object identity—for example Hodge class versus chosen cycle witness, surrogate coordinate versus root coordinate, or restricted source family versus full physical state.

**Potential regression:** overly rigid identity contracts could suppress legitimate replacement of one witness by another equivalent representative or make exploratory fibres cumbersome.

**Cheapest discriminating benchmark:** replay three existing application cases with the proposed interface contract:

1. this H4d1a class/witness hostile control;
2. P-vs-NP raw closure volume versus cover-cost coordinate;
3. Yang–Mills restricted source family versus full spectral-gap binding.

The framework hypothesis is **not** opened as a RAKL change from this single episode. It should accumulate support across independent episodes before Self-RAKL evaluation.
