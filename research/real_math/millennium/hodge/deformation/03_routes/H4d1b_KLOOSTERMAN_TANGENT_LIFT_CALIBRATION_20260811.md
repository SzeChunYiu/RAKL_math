# H4d1b source-bound first-order tangent-lift calibration

**Date:** 2026-08-11  
**Atom:** `H4d1b`  
**Source:** Remke Kloosterman, *Variational Hodge conjecture for complete intersections on hypersurfaces in projective space*, arXiv:2104.14845 / RSMUP (2022).  
**Authority:** `SOURCE_BOUND_METHOD_CALIBRATION / FIRST_ORDER_POSITIVE_CONTROL / NO_NEW_THEOREM / NO_ROOT_AUTHORITY`.

## Question

H4d1a showed that if the same Hodge/semiregularity detector `sigma` vanishes on all branch-generated first-order obstructions, then asking `sigma` to be injective on a nonzero branch image cannot repair the route. The next smaller question is geometric:

> Can an independently described Hodge-preserving base tangent be lifted directly to a first-order deformation of the chosen algebraic witness, so that the first-order branch obstruction vanishes without using detector injectivity?

This file answers **yes in Kloosterman's special complete-intersection source class**. It does not answer the question for arbitrary rational cycles.

## Source coordinates

Let

`Y = {F=0} subset P^{2k+1}`

be a smooth hypersurface of degree `e`, and let

`Z = {P_1=...=P_{k+1}=0} subset Y`

be a `k`-dimensional complete intersection of multidegree `(d_1,...,d_{k+1})` in the source range. Write

`F = sum_i P_i Q_i`,

with `deg(P_i)=d_i` and `deg(Q_i)=e-d_i`.

Kloosterman's proof associates to the primitive class of `Z` an Artinian-Gorenstein ideal `I` and, in this complete-intersection case, proves that

`I = (P_1,...,P_{k+1},Q_1,...,Q_{k+1})`

and that its degree-`e` piece is the tangent space to the Hodge locus at `Y`. His main theorem identifies the relevant Hodge-locus component with the locus of hypersurfaces containing a complete intersection of the same multidegree and proves smoothness at `Y`.

For H4d1b we use only the first-order source consequence.

## Explicit tangent preimage

Take an arbitrary first-order Hodge-preserving hypersurface deformation

`deltaF in T_Y NL([Z]_prim) = I_e`.

Because `I` is generated by the `P_i` and `Q_i`, degree compatibility gives forms

`deltaQ_i in S_{e-d_i}` and `deltaP_i in S_{d_i}`

such that

`deltaF = sum_i (P_i deltaQ_i + deltaP_i Q_i)`.

Work over the dual numbers `C[epsilon]/(epsilon^2)` and define

`P_i(epsilon) = P_i + epsilon deltaP_i`,  
`Q_i(epsilon) = Q_i + epsilon deltaQ_i`.

Then

`sum_i P_i(epsilon) Q_i(epsilon)
 = F + epsilon sum_i(P_i deltaQ_i + deltaP_i Q_i)
 = F + epsilon deltaF        mod epsilon^2`.

Therefore the first-order complete intersection

`Z_epsilon = {P_i(epsilon)=0 for all i}`

lies in the first-order hypersurface

`Y_epsilon = {F+epsilon deltaF=0}`.

Thus every source Hodge tangent has an explicit tangent preimage in the deformation space of pairs `(Y,Z)`. In H4d1b notation, the source-calibrated first-order obstruction map satisfies

`delta_1|_{T_Y NL([Z]_prim)} = 0`.

Equivalently, the branch-reachable first-order obstruction image is zero for this source class.

## Why this escapes H4d1a without contradiction

H4d1a asked whether the **same detector** could become faithful on a nonzero obstruction image already forced into its kernel. That mechanism is impossible at first order.

The present source calibration does not ask for detector faithfulness. It produces an explicit geometric preimage under the pair-to-base incidence differential. The old no-go is therefore a relevant warning that changes the representation, not a blacklist of the broader witness-lifting goal.

## Adversarial checks

### Check 1: post-hoc envelope
No obstruction envelope is defined from successful lifts. The Hodge tangent `I_e` and generators `(P_i,Q_i)` are supplied independently by the source.

**Verdict:** pass at source scope.

### Check 2: degree mismatch
The coefficient multiplying `P_i` in a degree-`e` element has degree `e-d_i`, while the coefficient multiplying `Q_i` has degree `d_i`. These are exactly the degrees required for `deltaQ_i` and `deltaP_i`.

**Verdict:** pass.

### Check 3: same-detector tautology
No injectivity of `sigma` is used.

**Verdict:** pass.

### Check 4: strict-weaker claim
Kloosterman explicitly notes that these complete-intersection cycles are semiregular in the source setting. The calibration therefore does **not** show that direct branch annihilation is weaker than full semiregularity in any realized nonsemiregular example.

**Verdict:** blocking for a strict-weaker claim.

### Check 5: higher order
The dual-number calculation contains no second-order term. Extending it through Artin thickenings requires a separately frozen deformation/obstruction analysis.

**Verdict:** open.

### Check 6: arbitrary rational cycles
The factorization `F=sum P_iQ_i` and ideal description are special complete-intersection coordinates.

**Verdict:** no transfer without a new witness.

## External controls

Bruzzo-Montoya provide a second special deformation-of-pairs/Noether-Lefschetz control in a toric setting: under their hypotheses, infinitesimal Hodge persistence for the class associated to the complete intersection is equivalent to the subvariety remaining algebraic, and locally the Noether-Lefschetz locus is an irreducible component of a suitable Hilbert scheme.

Movasati's cubic-hypersurface examples remain a hostile control. A selected triple can be rigid while the attached Hodge locus has positive dimension in studied cases, warning that Hodge tangent directions need not be exhausted by deformations of one chosen witness family.

## Result

**Verdict:** `KLOOSTERMAN_SOURCE_FIRST_ORDER_DIRECT_LIFT = PASS`.

This is a positive **source-bound method calibration**:
- a published special-family Hodge tangent is independently computed;
- every such tangent has an explicit incidence preimage;
- the first-order branch obstruction of that selected complete-intersection witness vanishes;
- no same-detector injectivity is needed.

It is not a new theorem, not a proof of the rational Hodge conjecture, and not a general variational-Hodge criterion.

## Residuals opened

### H4d1c — strict-weaker nonsemiregular calibration
Search for a source-bound example satisfying all of:
1. a nonzero Hodge-branch tangent is independently defined;
2. every tangent in that branch has a direct first-order witness lift;
3. the relevant full semiregularity map is independently non-injective or the source is independently nonsemiregular;
4. the lift is not obtained by defining the branch from successful witness deformations.

A positive example would demonstrate that direct branch annihilation can be genuinely weaker than full semiregularity. Failure would sharply bound the proposed weakening.

### H4d1b-HO — higher-Artin stability
Separately freeze the next-order deformation problem for the Kloosterman incidence mechanism. First-order tangent surjectivity must not be promoted to all-order formal lifting without exact obstruction data.

Formal algebraization, monodromy/global continuation and the Hodge root's initial-existence step remain downstream and open.
