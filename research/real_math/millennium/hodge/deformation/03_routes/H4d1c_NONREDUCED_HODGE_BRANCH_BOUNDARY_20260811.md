## Frozen question

The inherited H4d1c obstruction asks for an independent geometric mechanism forcing the chosen exact-category witness incidence map to be tangent-surjective onto a fixed Hodge-locus branch. Before theorem invention, the current v3 pre-action receipt froze a boundary test: is surjectivity onto the tangent space of the **full Hodge-locus scheme** actually required by the pointwise rational variational-Hodge objective when that scheme may be nonreduced or singular?

The exact lane remains a smooth projective complex family, rational class, fixed already algebraic finite signed rational central witness, and one local Hodge branch. Initial algebraicity and every higher-order/global obligation remain outside this atom.

## Boundary evidence

Dan's Noether-Lefschetz work supplies two facts in a solved/special control family. First, for complete-intersection classes the Hodge condition is equivalent to remaining algebraic, with the Hodge locus represented by a flag-Hilbert image. Second, in the surface/divisor case there are genuinely nonreduced Noether-Lefschetz/Hodge-locus components; Theorem 1.2 of arXiv:1404.7519 explicitly characterizes nonreduced examples. The independent paper arXiv:1407.8491 is devoted to criteria and examples for nonreduced Noether-Lefschetz components.

This makes the scheme-structure distinction non-vacuous inside smooth-projective Hodge geometry. In codimension one, pointwise rational `(1,1)` algebraicity is already controlled by Lefschetz `(1,1)`, while the Hodge-locus scheme can nevertheless possess nonreduced structure.

Separately, the scheme-theoretic tangent functor is defined using maps from dual numbers. The elementary model `Spec C[ε]/(ε²)` and its reduction `Spec C` have the same closed complex point but different infinitesimal structure; the former has a nonzero dual-number tangent direction while the latter does not. This toy model is not asserted to be Dan's local ring. It is only the exact falsifier showing that point support and full scheme tangent are different data.

## Result

The discriminator selects `REDUCED_BRANCH_ONLY_ROOT_ALIGNED`.

Unqualified surjectivity

`dπ : T_w W → T_s T_sch`

is **not a necessary formulation of the pointwise rational variational-Hodge target from the problem specification alone**. It asks the witness family to reproduce scheme-theoretic infinitesimal data that may include nilpotent thickening and, at singular points, tangent directions stronger than domination of actual nearby fibres.

The root-aligned geometric target should therefore be normalized first to the reduced support:

> Find an exact coefficient/category-preserving algebraic-cycle incidence component whose image dominates the irreducible reduced Hodge branch `T_red`.

Tangent surjectivity is retained as a sufficient local certificate when both the relevant source component and `T_red` are verified smooth at the chosen point. It is also a legitimate target in a **separately frozen scheme-theoretic infinitesimal-strengthening lane**. It is not silently imposed on the root-aligned pointwise lane.

This correction does **not** prove that a general witness-incidence component dominates `T_red`, and it does not exhibit an unliftable nilpotent tangent in Dan's examples. It only removes the unsupported equivalence between full Hodge-scheme tangent reachability and pointwise reduced-branch reachability.

## Proper-image refinement

If a globally defined exact-category witness parameter space is proper/projective over the relevant base and one irreducible component maps onto a dense open subset of an irreducible `T_red`, then its image is closed and dense, hence equals `T_red`. Thus a useful successor can work generically on `T_red^sm` and use properness/closure to cover singular points of the same reduced support.

This is only a route template. It requires an actual proper witness space, exact rational signed coefficient bookkeeping, and a component/dominance witness. A local analytic incidence object does not inherit properness by terminology.
