## Method used

The cycle used a counterexample-first interface audit rather than another
literature expansion. The frozen H4d1a representation was reduced to the exact
first-order maps it requires:

`delta_1 : T_{s0}S -> Ob_1`

and

`sigma : Ob_1 -> H_1`.

The branch-reachable envelope is
`B_{T,1}=delta_1(T_{s0}T)`. The parent route says Hodge persistence gives
`sigma(delta_1(v))=0` for `v in T_{s0}T`. These two statements imply
`B_{T,1} subset ker(sigma)`.

The falsifier asked whether the predeclared
`PROPER_NONZERO_AND_KERNEL_FREE` outcome can survive this compatibility
identity. It cannot.

## Role-separated same-context review

1. **VHS/Hodge-locus lead:** confirmed that the vanishing statement belongs to
   the branch compatibility relation, not to witness existence.
2. **Cycle/witness-moduli lead:** required `B` to be the image of an independent
   obstruction map rather than a post-hoc successful-lift set.
3. **Semiregularity/derived-obstruction lead:** rejected the idea that
   restricting the same detector to a nonzero subset of its kernel can restore
   faithfulness.
4. **Special-family lead:** caught a representation ambiguity in the frozen
   source addendum. Rigid witness tangent data do not by themselves imply a zero
   obstruction-image envelope.
5. **Adversarial lead:** rejected quotienting away the detector kernel as a
   tautological repair.
6. **Formal/novelty/learning-control lead:** classified the result as scoped
   route pruning and preserved all downstream obligations.

These are same-context analytical passes, not independent mathematical review.

## Outcome

`PARTIAL_SUCCESS / ROUTE_PRUNING`.

The first-order same-detector branch
`PROPER_NONZERO_AND_KERNEL_FREE` is formally impossible inside the registered
scope. A nonzero `B_{T,1}` necessarily gives a detector-kernel hit; detector
injectivity on the same envelope forces `B_{T,1}=0`.

This is useful negative research information. It removes a search branch that
the previous packet treated as potentially interesting.

## Residual

Open `H4d1b`: prove source-specifically that the branch-relative obstruction map
itself vanishes,
`delta_1|_{T_{s0}T}=0`, without assuming the desired witness lift or full
semiregularity. Only after a first-order source-bound result should a fresh
higher-Artin-order child be frozen.

Alternative-detector or different-obstruction-object mechanisms remain allowed,
but require fresh context and source binding.

## What failed

The process failure was **representation / relation / gluing**, not a failure of
the Hodge conjecture itself. The frozen packet treated a nonzero branch-reached
envelope and same-detector faithfulness as potentially compatible even though
the parent Hodge-persistence relation places that envelope inside the detector
kernel.

A second, narrower representation warning was found: “the chosen witness is
rigid” concerns witness deformation directions and does not automatically mean
the obstruction-image envelope is zero.

## Framework-improvement hypothesis

The episode suggests a narrow framework check for future representation-first
research: before accepting a candidate “restricted faithfulness” repair, test
whether the restricted set is already forced into the kernel of the proposed
detector by the target/context definition. This can be phrased as a generic
**constraint-detector compatibility audit**.

This is a framework-improvement hypothesis only. It should not become a
ResearchTool or RAKL method change until it transfers to fresh problems and
passes the normal protected gates.
