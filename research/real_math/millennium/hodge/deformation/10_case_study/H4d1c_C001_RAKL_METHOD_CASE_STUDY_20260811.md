## Research method and decision policy

The decision policy was adversarial boundary testing before theorem invention.

The cycle separated four obligations: fixed-witness infinitesimal reachability,
source-family directional reachability, controlled source-component/branch
coverage, and compatible higher-order/algebraic propagation. It then applied
two falsifiers.

First, the quantifier profiles differ: fixed-witness surjectivity has
`exists w0 / for every v`, while source-family directional existence can have
`for every v / exists w_v`.

Second, the structural DifferenceWitness `q(t)=t^2` is dominant/surjective on
complex points while its differential at the selected central source point is
zero. Thus source coverage does not imply tangent surjectivity at that selected
point.
