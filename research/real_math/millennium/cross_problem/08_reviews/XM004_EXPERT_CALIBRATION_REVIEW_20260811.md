## 1. Circuit and fusion complexity lead

**Background:** Boolean circuit lower bounds, discrete/fusion complexity, set-cover formulations.  
**Delegation:** verify the exact source direction of `rho`, `D_intersection`, and Theorem 24 before any lower-bound interpretation.

Finding: Theorem 22 gives `rho(A,B) <= D_intersection(A|B)`, while Theorem 24 supplies the converse-direction upper construction `D_intersection(A|B) <= rho(A,B)^2`. The `G_w` machinery lives inside this upper construction. Therefore closure richness has no automatic lower-bound direction. The all-rectangle example is especially hostile because the source itself states `rho(G,R_{N,N})=0` for every nontrivial graph.

Objection to group: do not turn the exact closure derivation into an alleged lower-bound theorem merely because `G_w` looks structurally richer than C025 signatures.

## 2. Combinatorics and closure-systems lead

**Background:** closure operators, Horn propagation, finite set systems.  
**Delegation:** determine whether the proposed cheap target truly produces maximal closure without paid propagation.

Finding: for `a=(i,j) in G`, the singleton rectangle `{i}x{j}` is in the source generator family. With `U=G^c`, its trace is empty. The source base rule inserts that trace and all supersets. Since every subset of `U` contains the empty set, the closure is immediately `P(U)`. No pair-index propagation is required.

Objection to group: raw number of present sets, raw entropy, and upward-closure depth are contaminated by generator choice. A future score must separate free seeding/upward saturation from pair-indexed productive events.

## Deliberation and consensus

The circuit lead and closure lead initially differed on how strong the negative conclusion could be. The adversarial lead resolved the disagreement by distinguishing **raw state membership** from **productive pair-index activation**. The transfer lead then checked that this distinction is exactly the DifferenceWitness needed to avoid blacklisting the entire closure method family. The assurance lead constrained the authority because of retrospective chronology; the novelty lead constrained the claim to program pruning.

**Consensus:**

- register `F-XM004-PNP-UPWARD-CLOSURE-VOLUME-INFLATION` at `SUPPORTED`;
