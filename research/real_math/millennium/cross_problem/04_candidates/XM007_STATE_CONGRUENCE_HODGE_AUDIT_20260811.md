## Abstract operation

Let `(X, ->)` be a transition system and `pi:X->Y` a proposed compressed state. Let `Reach(x)` denote the registered downstream reachability object, or replace it by the exact outcome predicate used by the target task.

A necessary condition for the claim

```text
"pi(x) contains all state needed for the downstream process"
```

is that states collapsed by `pi` cannot be distinguished by the registered downstream outcome. In its strongest reachable-set form,

```text
pi(x)=pi(x')  =>  projected Reach(x) = projected Reach(x').
```

For a binary target predicate `Q`, the weaker necessary condition is

```text
pi(x)=pi(x')  =>  Q(x)=Q(x').
```

Therefore a single collision

```text
pi(x)=pi(x') but Q(x) != Q(x')
```

falsifies `pi` as a sufficient state for `Q`. This statement is elementary and domain-independent. What is domain-specific is the choice of `X`, `pi`, the allowed transitions, and `Q`.

### Primary-source positive controls

- Takeo Nishinou, *Deformation of pairs and semiregularity*, arXiv:2009.01651. Under the paper's semiregularity condition for divisor maps into a family of Kähler manifolds, relative deformation is equivalent to persistence of the image cycle class as a Hodge class. This is a setting where a strong source theorem supplies the missing sufficiency bridge.
- Remke Kloosterman, *Variational Hodge conjecture for complete intersections on hypersurfaces in projective space*, arXiv:2104.14845. This gives another scoped positive class in which the variational Hodge problem is solved for complete-intersection cycles on hypersurfaces.

These are not evidence that the same state coordinates are sufficient in arbitrary Hodge problems. They are positive calibration worlds in which strong hypotheses make class persistence informative about algebraic-witness deformation.

### Primary-source hostile calibration

Hossein Movasati, *Hodge cycles for cubic hypersurfaces*, arXiv:1902.00831, chooses a smooth deformation space in which the original selected linear-cycle witness triple is rigid, while specified Hodge loci can nevertheless be smooth, reduced and positive-dimensional in the studied cases. This does not provide the exact equal-`pi` collision needed for the future H4d1a1 test because `pi` has not yet been frozen. It does establish why `Hodge locus moves` cannot be silently identified with `the originally selected witness moves`.

## Prospective Hodge falsifier

Once H4d1a1 freezes `W`, `pi` and an exact downstream outcome, run this before another detector score or theorem search:

1. Define the exact equivalence `w1 ~ w2` iff `pi(w1)=pi(w2)`.
2. Define the target outcome, for example existence of an admissible relative witness deformation, a specified tangent-lift condition, or vanishing/nonvanishing of the registered obstruction.
3. Search source-bound positive and hostile families for `w1,w2` with equal projection but different target outcome.
4. If one collision exists, reject `pi` as sufficient state and refine it with source-defined representative identity, replacement-witness class, tangent/obstruction or another missing coordinate.
5. If no collision is found, that alone is not proof of sufficiency. Retain `pi` only if the bounded search plus a source theorem or separately proved congruence establishes the required scope.
6. If the target state/outcome cannot be instantiated from sources, record `CANNOT_CHECK` instead of treating the absence of a counterexample as a pass.

This is cheaper than optimizing a detector over an under-specified state and discovering later that the state collapsed two geometrically distinct witness futures.
