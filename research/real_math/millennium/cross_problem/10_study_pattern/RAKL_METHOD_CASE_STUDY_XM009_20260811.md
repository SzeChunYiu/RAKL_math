## 4. Cross-lane empirical diagnosis

Across these six representative cycles, the strongest shared method pattern is **not** “always change representation.” It is a two-stage discipline:

1. expose the exact root-facing coordinate that a representation must preserve or make nondegenerate; then
2. audit the local-to-global consumer/gluing interface independently.

PNP and Hodge show representation correction can prune overrich or saturated coordinates. YM and RH show local/source/aggregate information can fail because the consumer quantifier/state family is wider. NS shows a local representation bridge can close while the producer/global interface remains open. BSD shows a richer representation can clarify the frontier while leaving the first root-facing relation absent.

The positive motif is therefore `REPRESENTATION_CHANGE -> ROOT_COORDINATE_NONDEGENERACY -> CONSUMER/GLUING_AUDIT`, not representation change by itself. XM009 adds the negative-control half: quotienting a nuisance baseline can be mathematically correct yet scientifically useless if the target coordinate becomes identically flat.
