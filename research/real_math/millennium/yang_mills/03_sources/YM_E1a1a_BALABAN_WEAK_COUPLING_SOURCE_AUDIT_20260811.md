## Primary-source scope

### Balaban 1985 — background fields

T. Bałaban, *The variational problem and background fields in renormalization group method for lattice gauge theories*, Commun. Math. Phys. **102** (1985), 277–309. DOI `10.1007/BF01229381`.

The paper treats regular lattice gauge configurations with fixed averages, proves existence of an action minimum unique up to gauge transformations, and uses the resulting background field as the basis for expansion/perturbative methods.

**Transfer:** formulate the marked block in the same coarse-variable/background-field geometry.

**Non-transfer:** background-field existence/uniqueness is not a source-tangent or observable-closure theorem.

### Balaban 1987 — 4D small-field one-step RG

T. Bałaban, *Renormalization group approach to lattice gauge field theories. I. Generation of effective actions in a small field approximation and a coupling constant renormalization in four dimensions*, Commun. Math. Phys. **109** (1987), 249–301. DOI `10.1007/BF01215223`.

The paper studies four-dimensional pure gauge theory in the small-field approximation and constructs localized effective actions by cluster expansions in one-step RG transformations, together with recursive coupling renormalization.

**Transfer:** test the mark against the actual one-step localization/blocking structure and running weak coupling.

**Non-transfer:** an unmarked effective-action induction does not automatically control differentiated sources, composite insertions or loop geometry.

### Balaban 1988 — cluster-expansion induction

T. Bałaban, *Renormalization group approach to lattice gauge field theories. II. Cluster expansions*, Commun. Math. Phys. **116** (1988), 1–22. DOI `10.1007/BF01239022`.

The fluctuation-field integral from Part I is represented by an exponentiated cluster expansion, and the expansion terms are shown to satisfy the inductive assumptions, completing the small-field effective-action sequence.

**Transfer:** marked observables should be organized at compatible localization/polymer granularity if their estimates are to compose with the base induction.

**Non-transfer:** density closure does not say a source derivative remains inside any finite marked vocabulary.

### Balaban 1989 — large-field R operation

T. Bałaban, *Large field renormalization. I. The basic step of the R operation*, Commun. Math. Phys. **122** (1989), 175–202. DOI `10.1007/BF01257412`.

T. Bałaban, *Large field renormalization. II. Localization, exponentiation, and bounds for the R operation*, Commun. Math. Phys. **122** (1989), 355–392. DOI `10.1007/BF01238433`.

These papers construct and bound the large-field `R` operation used to address the large-field obstruction in the four-dimensional ultraviolet analysis.
