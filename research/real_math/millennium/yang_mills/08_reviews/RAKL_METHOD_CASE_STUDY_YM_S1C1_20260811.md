1. **Root-coordinate preservation prevented local-success inflation.** The audit separated a fixed-cutoff transfer gap from the much stronger Wilson bare-coupling/continuum bridge.
2. **Counterexample-first algebra was cheap and decisive at the source-claim level.** No simulation or new theorem was needed: the displayed interval for `beta_K` has an immediate `K->infinity` consequence.
3. **V3 fibre composition helped avoid redundant work.** PR #46 and PR #62 made another volume or abstract spectral pass low-information.
4. **Gluing language localized the real failure.** The fixed-cutoff sections can remain locally valid while the `bare_coupling_escape` interface stays open.
5. **Chronology discipline prevented backfilled authority.** The result was found before the new atom was frozen, so it is stored only as retrospective experience.

## Concrete framework-improvement hypothesis

### `FRESH_SOURCE_COLLISION_GATE`

**Target RAKL component:** problem-fibre compilation / search policy.

**Hypothesis:** when an active atom has a named residual and a newly available primary source claims to close the same residual, the fibre compiler should elevate a source-collision audit above theorem invention. The compiled fibre should bind:
- publication/revision date;
- exact claimed root interface;
- source theorem IDs;
- coordinate map from source variables to target/root variables;
- cheapest source-internal falsifier.

**Expected benefit:** less duplicate theorem invention and faster detection of hidden bridge assumptions.

**Possible regression:** over-prioritizing noisy or low-quality new preprints could consume search budget.

**Cheapest benchmark:** replay this YM-S1c1 episode plus at least two controls:
1. a fresh paper whose claimed bridge survives the audit and correctly changes route priority;
2. a sensational but irrelevant fresh paper that should be rejected by DifferenceWitness/scope before deep reading.

The improvement remains a hypothesis until repeated cross-lane episodes support it.
