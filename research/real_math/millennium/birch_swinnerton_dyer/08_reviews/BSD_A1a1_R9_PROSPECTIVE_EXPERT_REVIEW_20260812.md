## Cell discussion and decision
The cell considered three routes: (A) treat analytic rank two as rank-two Euler-system degree over Q; (B) base-change to K and use the rank-two Heegner carrier; (C) use current discrete/Kolyvagin nonvanishing results as a substitute. Route A is falsified by the source definition `r_T=1` over Q. Route B is mathematically meaningful but does not close the root: the available construction and derivative/leading-term theorem cells import a Heegner main conjecture, positive algebraic ranks, p-primary Sha finiteness, a conjectural complex leading-term identity, and regulator nonvanishing. Route C is rejected by coordinate mismatch.

Consensus outcome: **PARTIAL_SUCCESS_ROUTE_PRUNED_NO_ROOT_CANDIDATE**. The local Kataoka-Sano theorems remain valid; the failure is representation/gluing, not a local mathematical contradiction. The residual is narrowed to finding a theorem that maps exact complex analytic rank two over Q to a root-faithful arithmetic nonvanishing/rank carrier under genuinely weaker hypotheses, before entering higher-rank Euler-system or regulator machinery.
