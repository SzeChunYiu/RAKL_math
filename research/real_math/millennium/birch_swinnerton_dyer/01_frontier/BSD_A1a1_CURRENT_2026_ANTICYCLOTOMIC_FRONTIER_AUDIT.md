## Fresh primary-source frontier scan

### Burungale–Kobayashi–Nakamura–Ota, arXiv:2608.06879

The paper *Anticyclotomic Iwasawa theory of CM elliptic curves at ramified primes* was submitted on 7 August 2026.  It develops an integral anticyclotomic Iwasawa theory for **CM elliptic curves** when `p` is **ramified in the CM field**, defines signed Selmer groups using Lagrangian local conditions, and proves an integral Iwasawa main conjecture relating one signed Selmer group to a p-adic L-function.  The p-adic L-function interpolates central Hecke L-values of anticyclotomic twists with epsilon sign `+1`, including arbitrary infinity type, and values in the `-1` sign family are related to p-adic logarithms of Selmer elements.

This is a substantial new near-solved context, but it does not close `BSD-A1a1` for three independent reasons.

1. Its arithmetic scope is special: CM elliptic curves and primes ramified in the CM field.
2. The analytic variable being interpolated is still the anticyclotomic twist/character family.  The theorem does not identify the order of the complex `s`-Taylor expansion of `L(E,s)` at `s=1` with the exact augmentation order at the trivial anticyclotomic character.
3. An Iwasawa main conjecture identifies p-adic analytic and Selmer characteristic information at its stated arithmetic strength; it is not by itself a theorem that bare complex analytic rank two forces the required p-adic regulator/height nondegeneracy.

The result therefore enlarges local p-adic machinery while preserving the live cross-direction relation gap.

Primary source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06879`.

### Longo–Pati–Vigni, arXiv:2603.22483

The paper *Anticyclotomic Iwasawa main conjectures for modular forms* proves definite and indefinite anticyclotomic main conjectures, plus a BDP/Brooks p-adic-L-function main conjecture when `p` splits in `K`, under its stated hypotheses.  Its modular form has **even weight at least 4**.  Hence it is a useful structural analogue for anticyclotomic main-conjecture technology but it is not the weight-two elliptic-curve theorem required by the present BSD atom.

Even abstracting away the weight mismatch, a main-conjecture equality in an anticyclotomic deformation coordinate does not itself transport the complex `s`-derivative order at `s=1` into that coordinate.  It therefore remains a method-transfer analogue, not the missing bridge.

Primary source: `https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.22483`.

### Castella–Hsieh and higher-rank Selmer machinery

Castella–Hsieh (`arXiv:1809.09066`, later Forum Math. Sigma 10 (2022)) remains the key local rank-two source in the existing packet.  Its generalized-Kato leading-term framework relates anticyclotomic p-adic leading information to derived p-adic heights and an enhanced p-adic regulator, but the exact theta order / positive-rank hypotheses remain load-bearing.  It does not turn exact complex analytic rank two into the exact anticyclotomic order needed by the route.

Chan-Ho Kim (`arXiv:2203.12161`) gives higher Gross–Zagier/Selmer-structure refinements and arbitrary-rank arithmetic structure without a low analytic-rank hypothesis in the structural results, but the source does not supply the universal complex-rank-two-to-anticyclotomic-order theorem sought here.  This is a strong arithmetic near-miss rather than root glue.
