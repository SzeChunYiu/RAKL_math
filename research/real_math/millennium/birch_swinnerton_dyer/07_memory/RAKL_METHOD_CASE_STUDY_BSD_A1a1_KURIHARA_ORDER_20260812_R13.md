## What worked

The same-domain primary-source discriminator worked. Kim/Pollack prove a strong discrete classifier: in scope, Kurihara order equals Bloch–Kato Selmer corank, and a single nonzero Kurihara number at index `n` yields `corank Sel <= ν(n)`. This exposed a strictly weaker sufficient target than the full complex/discrete equality: a nonzero witness with `ν(n)=2`, combined with a separately verified same-theory lower bound `>=2`, would force exact Selmer corank two.

The source audit also gave a decisive falsifier. The generic equality of complex Taylor order and Kurihara discrete order is explicitly **Conjecture 1.6**, while the proved equality with complex order in Corollary 1.10 stops at analytic rank at most one. Corollary 1.5 gives parity only. This prevents a subtle but consequential rank-two overtransfer.

Operationally, RAKL's separation of representation translation from gluing was useful: the local Kurihara theorem cell succeeds, while the root-facing entry edge and the same-theory coordinate composition are still open. The shadow admission model also prevented the new episode or failure record from acquiring canonical lesson/tool/proof authority merely by being stored.

## What failed

No theorem was found in the audited primary source that derives Kurihara discrete order two—or even existence of a nonzero two-prime Kurihara witness—from exact complex analytic rank two. The failure is **not** a local mathematical failure: the local theorem is valid. It is primarily a **representation/gluing/source-scope failure**: the complex analytic carrier has not been proved to enter the discrete carrier at the sharp order needed by the root-facing consumer. A separate same-theory weight-two coefficient/lattice/local-condition binding with the R9 Selmer object remains to be checked before any inequality-squeeze path is credited.

The low-rank analogue does not transfer: Corollary 1.10 explicitly assumes analytic rank `<=1`. The parity theorem does not produce an upper bound. Numerical Kurihara witnesses can calibrate or certify individual scoped cases but cannot prove the generic analytic-rank-two implication. Full BSD gluing—Mordell–Weil rank, Sha, regulator, local/Tamagawa factors, torsion, periods and the complex leading term—remains untouched.
