## What worked

The strongest process gain was **ontology normalization before further retrieval**. The phrase “higher Gross–Zagier” was decomposed into three independent coordinates: higher complex derivative order, higher weight/cycle codimension, and higher p-adic deformation derivative order. This prevented three strong-looking but invalid transfers from being merged.

The bounded search found a genuine solved structural analogue: Yun–Zhang identify an `r`-th complex central derivative with a Heegner–Drinfeld self-intersection over function fields. That result changed the representation of the search target from “some p-adic nonvanishing theorem” to “a root-faithful arithmetic carrier of complex derivative order two.” A DifferenceWitness blocked theorem transfer because the source geometry is function-field/Shtuka rather than number-field elliptic-curve arithmetic.

The current Castella–Hsieh manuscript provided a decisive target-near falsifier. It explicitly replaces the motivating analytic-rank condition by an algebraic counterpart; the analytic-rank-two equivalence belongs to the Darmon–Rotger conjecture, and its Corollary B assumes Mordell–Weil rank two plus finite p-primary Sha. This prevents circular use as the missing analytic bridge.
